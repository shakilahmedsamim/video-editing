# Hook library

The first 3 to 12 seconds. Rule from the PDF playbook: show a business problem within 10 s, introduce yourself after.
Every hook below is already built as a scene you can copy (file in brackets). No em dashes, no invented numbers:
any number on screen is labeled DEMO DATA unless the user gave the real one.

Other proven openers to write next time (not built yet, use the same scene recipe):
- **Before / after** split: "Last month: 31 tracked. After the fix: every order recorded." (only with real evidence)
- **Myth**: "A tag firing is not a conversion." (big text, strike-through on "tag fired")
- **Cost of the problem**: "Every untracked lead is budget your ads cannot learn from."
- **Direct call-out**: "Running Google Ads on Shopify? Watch this before you trust your ROAS."

## Hook checklist

- Problem first, name second. Name lower-third or intro by about 0:12.
- One idea per beat, on screen for at least 2 s, readable on a phone.
- First visual change within 0.5 s (no logo intro).
- SFX on every reveal in the promo style (pop, thud on the problem, whoosh on the switch).
