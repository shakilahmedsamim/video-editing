Create a step by step screen walkthrough video for a marketing agency owner. He will use it to show his client exactly where to click, platform by platform, to find the event tracking that is live on the website etheorie.be. The video must feel like someone is guiding him live through each screen: a cursor moves to the right place, clicks, the next screen appears, and a short caption says what to click and what to show the client.

17 screenshots are attached, numbered 01 to 17. They are real screens from the accounts. Use them exactly as they are. Do not redraw, edit or invent any interface. Some areas are already blurred on purpose. Leave them blurred.

All screenshots are 1568 x 734 pixels. Every coordinate below is in pixels on the original screenshot, measured from the top left corner.

# Look and feel

- Apple style. Light grey background, #F5F5F7. Clean sans serif type such as Inter or SF Pro. Lots of space.
- Each screenshot sits in a large rounded card, corner radius about 18 px, with a soft shadow, filling most of the frame.
- A thin breadcrumb bar sits above the card and shows the click path so far, for example: Google Tag Manager › EtheoRie.be › Versions. Update it on every step.
- A small round step badge in the top left of the frame shows the step number, for example: Step 3.
- A caption bar sits below the card. It has two lines: a bold action line, for example Click Versions, and a smaller grey line that starts with Show your client, followed by what to point out.
- Use one accent color for everything you highlight: Apple blue, #0071E3.

# How each step is animated

1. The screenshot fades in. If the previous step used the same screenshot, keep it on screen and only move the camera.
2. A macOS style arrow cursor glides smoothly to the click point given in the step.
3. On the click, a soft blue ripple expands from the cursor and the clicked element gets a rounded blue outline.
4. The camera gently zooms toward the area that matters, then a spotlight dims the rest of the screenshot to about 40 percent so the highlighted area stands out.
5. Hold for 3 to 5 seconds so the viewer can read, then move to the next step.

Keep all motion slow and smooth with ease in and ease out. No flashy transitions, no emojis, no sound effects other than a very soft click.

# Text rules

- English only.
- Never use em dashes or en dashes anywhere, not in captions, not in titles, not in the voiceover. Use a comma, a colon or a new line instead.
- No quotation marks, brackets or parentheses in on screen text.
- Never show or say an email address, a date or an account ID number.

# Voiceover

Add a calm, friendly English voiceover that reads the Say line of each step. If voiceover is not possible, show the Say line as subtitles.

# Output

1920 x 1080, 30 fps, MP4. Total length about 3 to 4 minutes.
